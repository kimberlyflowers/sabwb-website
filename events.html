Time to Rise Conference

May 15 — 16, 2026 San Antonio, Texas 200+ Attendees Expected

About This Event

The Time to Rise Conference is a powerful two-day gathering bringing together leaders, pastors, and influencers from across the nation who are committed to confronting the persecution of Christians and standing boldly for international religious freedom.

Join us in San Antonio, Texas for two transformative days of worship, powerful speakers, strategic conversations, and community building. Whether you're a pastor, business leader, educator, or civic servant — this event is designed to equip and empower you to rise up and make a difference.
