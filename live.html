About This Conference

The Time to Rise Conference brings together leaders, pastors, and influencers from across the nation who are committed to confronting the persecution of Christians and standing boldly for international religious freedom. Presented by Outpouring Missions International with Africa Arise USA and the Save Nigeria Group.

"The Diaspora Rises for the Indigenous" — Join us for two powerful days of worship, strategic conversations, and commissioning as we stand for the persecuted church worldwide.
